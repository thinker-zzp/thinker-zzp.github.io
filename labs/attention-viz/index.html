About Self-Attention

In Transformer models, Self-Attention dynamically weights relationships between words in a sequence:

Attn(Q,K,V) = softmax(QKT / √dk) V

Multi-Head Attention lets the model jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces (syntactic, coreference, and locality).
