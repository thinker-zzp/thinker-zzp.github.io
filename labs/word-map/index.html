About Word Embeddings

Words are mapped to dense continuous vectors in high-dimensional space. The semantic distance is measured by Cosine Similarity:

cos(θ) = (u · v) / (||u|| · ||v||)

Analogies like "King − Man + Woman ≈ Queen" illustrate vector arithmetic in this hand-crafted example space.
